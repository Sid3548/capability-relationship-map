Llama-3.1-8B int8 causal-ablation pilot — protocol handoff

Protocol state: draft 0.1, evidence audit in progress. No GPU experiment has started. This document will be frozen and hashed before held-out intervention outcomes are examined.

Scope lock. Only the coding- and translation-conditioned Llama pilot through 10.0% is authorized. Qwen and the complete 15-target/0–50% experiment are prohibited.

Research question and classification

Does cumulative intervention on globally lowest-ranked MLP channels, where ranking is independently conditioned on coding or translation localization prompts, reveal reproducible target dependence or directed collateral degradation across a fixed 15-capability battery? This is causal capability-conditioned lesion mapping using structured activation ablation, not structural pruning.

Claims are limited to performance dependence under the specified intervention and the MLP-channel basis. The study will not claim that a channel stores or contains a capability, nor that the intervention exposes the model's complete algorithm.

Model and intervention unit

Checkpoint: local base D:\hf_models\Meta-Llama-3.1-8B, loaded offline with int8 weight quantization. The provisional unit is one channel of h = SiLU(gate_proj(x)) * up_proj(x) immediately before down_proj. Expected dimensions are 32 layers × 14,336 channels = 458,752 units; live inspection must confirm the module path, dimensions, dtypes, device placement, and absence of CPU offload.

Scoring and data integrity

Prompt and answer are tokenized jointly; loss is restricted to gold-answer tokens; items are macro-averaged so answer length does not control capability means. Padding side, attention masks, boundary cases, per-token outputs, answer-token counts, and parse status are persisted. The sealed 600 cannot influence ranking, metric choice, thresholds, prompts, controls, or checkpoint selection.

Ranking stability and comparators

Disjoint localization halves and prompt bootstraps will yield layerwise Spearman correlations and top-5%/top-10% Jaccard overlaps. Provisional gates are Spearman ≥0.80 and top-k Jaccard ≥0.60. Comparators at preregistered sentinels include activation×outgoing-weight magnitude, technically reliable Taylor scoring if feasible, high-importance, random layer-matched (at least ten deterministic seeds if runtime permits), global activity, and shuffled-target controls.

Interventions and sentinel controls

At 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, and 10%, compare calibration-mean replacement with zero replacement and feasible in-distribution/resampling replacement. Include empty-mask/no-op, high-importance, global, shuffled, and layer-matched random masks. If mean, zero, and resampling reverse the qualitative conclusion, causal interpretation stops.

Integrity gates before dense execution

  1. No-hook, empty-mask, and uninstrumented NLL/logits agree within declared tolerance.
  2. Repeated seeded runs are deterministic.
  3. Mask nesting, exact counts, indices, and hashes validate at all 100 states.
  4. Gold-token masks pass unit tests and hand inspection.
  5. The sealed 600 remains inaccessible to ranking and protocol adaptation.
  6. All capability baselines are usable, parse failure is below the frozen limit, and closed-choice tasks are above chance.
  7. Coding and translation functional baselines are strong enough to measure degradation.
  8. Int8 placement, quantized modules, post-gate hook location, and dtypes are verified with no unexpected CPU offload.
  9. A tiny timed smoke sweep produces the exact expected row counts and resumes safely.

Statistical plan

The prompts, not the 101 correlated intervention states, are the main independent evaluation units. Analysis uses paired prompt bootstrap, calibration-ranking bootstrap, random-mask uncertainty, curve and breakpoint intervals, area under ΔNLL/retention curves, early-damage summaries, target-versus-nontarget specificity, and directed target→capability effects. Direction-level translation estimates (four items per direction) are explicitly exploratory.

Translation design

Languages: English, German, Spanish, Hindi, Arabic, and Mandarin Chinese. The held-out translation set has four items for each of ten English↔other-language directions. Reports separate overall, source-language, target-language, direction, script-family, English-dependence, and generic-collapse views.

Generic-collapse and quantization gates

A generic language-model monitor and all-capability macro outcome will define broad model damage before results are viewed. Int8 fidelity will compare a meaningful BF16/FP16 subset where feasible; suggested gates are ≤2 accuracy points or ≤5% relative NLL, ranking Spearman ≥0.85, and top-k overlap ≥0.70. If full higher-precision loading is infeasible, conclusions remain explicitly int8-scoped.
