Honest answer to "when do they fire?" Only 0.1% are effectively dead (mean_abs < 0.02 on ALL four tasks) — they are NOT junk neurons. They are low-activation specifically for coding (mean_abs 0.0572, below the 0.0911 global median) but fire at or above the global median for the other tasks (math 0.0928, history 0.1175, reasoning 0.0837). 94.9% of them fire more strongly on some other task than on coding. So the coding envelope is "neurons coding does not rely on because they are busy serving other tasks," not "dead weight." That is why removing them is safe for coding yet shows collateral on other tasks in the interlink matrix.
